# research-loop (one run, a few minutes)

Never upload, post, spend paid credits or change channel settings. The owner decides those.

1. Read the channel's focus file (threads to rotate, e.g. audience pain points · fresh videos in the niche · news).
2. Take the thread that ran longest ago. One thread per run.
3. Evidence rules: a fact goes on screen only with two independent sources (two papers quoting one wire = one source). Aggregator posts are leads, not sources — find the official page. Write down what you could **not** confirm.
4. Small-channel breakouts teach more than giant channels: views ÷ subscribers, this month. Keep lookups low and slow; stop at the first rate-limit.
5. Append dated findings to the research log; add each new candidate as one backlog row: topic · format · evidence link · what the owner must do (record, decide).
6. If a candidate is strong and needs nothing from the owner, build a free draft and look at its frames.
7. End with a three-line summary. If you corrected an earlier mistake, say so in the log.
